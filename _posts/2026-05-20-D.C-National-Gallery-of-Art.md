> A personal reflection on several artworks that caught my eye during my visit to the National Gallery of Art in Washington, D.C.

Introduction

I visited the National Gallery of Art in Washington, D.C. after spending the day in D.C. Instead of trying to cover everything I saw, I decided to write about several artworks that stayed with me.

国家美术馆的藏品实在太丰富了，我无法一一罗列，而且去的时间也接近close time；看得太匆忙，我只好把让我最shocking的画作拍下来；或者有的看不懂，但是第一眼觉得背后肯定有很多典故和玄机；有的只是看着它，感受到了画中人的痛苦。（我没有拍下任何sculpture类艺术，目前我对于sculpture的理解甚为肤浅，我不知道雕刻背后是否有着和画作一样的典故，所以没有include它们）

Rather than organizing the works chronologically, I group them into three themes:

- Women, Beauty, and the Female Body （女性身体如何被描绘，画家如何通过画作教会我们如何观看女人的身体，观看女人的痛苦）

# I. Women, Beauty, and the Female Body

This section focuses on how women and female bodies are represented, idealized, exoticized, disciplined, or reimagined across different periods of art history.

## 1. *Venus with a Mirror* — Titian

**Artist:** Titian  
**Date:** c. 1555  
**Medium:** Oil on canvas  
**Museum:** National Gallery of Art, Washington, D.C.

![Alt Text](venus_with_a_mirror_1937.1.34.jpg){: w="300" }

> “男人观看女人，女人看着自己被观看." - John Berger

### What I noticed first

Two Cupids surround Venus (naked, ofc). One holds an mirror and she is looking into the mirror to observe herself. What is her goal of looking? To ensure she is beautiful enough? What does Cupid represent in that period of art? What is the meaning of mirror?

### Background

Titian (提香) was one of the most influential painter in Italian Reneinasse. Titian's genius lay in his revolutionary handling of color and brushwork. While Florentine and Roman artists like Michelangelo emphasized disegno (drawing and line), Titian championed colorito — building forms through layered color, atmospheric light, and visible, expressive brushstrokes. In his later years (the period of Venus with a Mirror), his technique became increasingly free and painterly, with surfaces that seem to shimmer and dissolve up close but cohere into vivid, living flesh from a distance.
维纳斯在文艺复兴时期的象征：
    - ideal beauty：physical body as a divine harmony; 她的身体成为画家探索人体完美比例与优雅的spectacle
    - Sensuality and love: 

### My reflection

Venus凝视着镜子，镜子中的她回望着她。她是否知道自己是美的？她是带着怎样的目光去观看自己？审视？欣赏？自恋？虚荣？作为美的女神凝视着自己的美，她意识到自己的美。我们作为观者又该如何欣赏这幅画？画家是否在邀请我们，如何欣赏一个美的女人？

The image of the mirror in the Renaissance art:
represent sinful vanity and nobel truth:
Venus gazing mirror, conflating the mirror of beauty, sexuality, and the sin of vanity; mirror-gazing also means diametrically opposed value - justice, humility and prudence. --- Vision, Vanitas, and Veritas: [The Mirror in Art](https://newprairiepress.org/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1733&context=sttcl) by Helena Goscilo

## 2. *Parau na te Varua ino (Words of the Devil)* — Paul Gauguin

**Artist:** Paul Gauguin  
**Date:** 1892  
**Medium:** Oil on canvas  
**Museum:** National Gallery of Art, Washington, D.C.

![Alt Image](parau_na_te_varua_ino_words_of_the_devil_1972.9.12.jpg)

### What I noticed first

A naked woman (non-white, unknown ethnicity) and a well-dressed male behind her. What was the facial expression on the woman? shy? self-esteem? 第一眼以为作者是在从被殖民者的角度解读亚当和夏娃的故事。

### Background

Paul Gauguin (法) 在Tahiti旅游期间创作了这幅画，他前往塔希提的原因是寻找“去工业化”的异域社会。He envisioned the place as a primitive paradise isolated from European modern civilization, capitalism and urban life. But when he went there, Tahiti was colonized by French already. 

### My reflection

画面中的女人是塔希提当地女人，但她遮挡身体的姿势让人轻易联想到西方古典雕塑中的 Venus Pudica；女性用手遮挡身体，但这种何当反而强化了观看者对身体的注意。她也让人联想到Eve：裸体，羞耻，诱惑，原罪。

这幅画已不再是纯粹塔希提女性，她被Gauguin放置在了欧洲白人男性熟悉的视觉系统里. Her voice was merely heard even though was placed at the center of the frame; it was Gauguin who was depicting, portraying 神化，决定观众如何看她。画中的女性即是身体，也是符号；她被观看，被命名，也被殖民性的幻想包裹。

Titian的Venus是欧洲文艺复兴中被理想化的女性美，Gauguin的塔希提女性是被异域化，被神秘化的美；两者都不是“自然的女性身体”，而是被艺术，欲望和文化制度制造出来的美。（那真正的女性美是什么样呢？我无法想象）

## 3. *afro.died, T.* — Rozeal

**Artist:** Rozeal  
**Date:** 2011  
**Medium:** Acrylic on canvas  
**Museum:** National Gallery of Art, Washington, D.C.


### Background

Rozeal. is a contemporary black artist born in DC. She is famous for her attention on black culture, Japanese visual culture, hip-hop and fashion. 

她的作品很有代表性的特点是 mash-up：把日本浮世绘 Ukiyo-e 的平面化线条、姿态、装饰性，与 African American / hip-hop / 当代流行文化结合起来。一些画廊介绍也把她的风格描述为传统 Ukiyo-e aesthetics 与 contemporary hip hop culture 的混合。

### My reflection

这幅画最eye-catching的点是画中女性的皮肤是黑色和白色混在一起，这种深色颜料好像在皮肤上流动，攀爬。But if you gaze twice, you will consider: 人是在褪色，还是在被上色？她是在变成另一个身份，还是某种被制造出来的身份正在脱落？

The National Gallery of Art gave me a chance to see how different artists, across centuries and cultures, used the human body to tell stories about faith, beauty, violence, power, and memory.
